The atelier

Tested on the Karakoram Highway

Xiks Collection is a men's wear shop on the KKH in Hussaini, Gojal Hunza — where winter means −10°C mornings, gale-force valley winds and a road that eats cheap clothing. So we stock what survives it: layered 2-in-1 jackets, waterproof shells, thick knitwear and track sets. Premium quality, winter ready, built to last — that isn't a slogan here, it's the minimum.

  • Waterproof & windproof — 2-in-1 shells you wear together or as separate layers.
  • Every piece checked by hand in Hussaini before it is packed.
  • DM to order, cash on delivery — no card, no account, no hassle.
